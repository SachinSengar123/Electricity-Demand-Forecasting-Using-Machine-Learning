Project Mission & Scope

Problem Statement:

Electricity demand changes throughout the day due to weather conditions, consumer behavior, industrial activities, and other factors. Predicting future demand helps improve electricity planning and resource allocation.

Proposed Solution:

GridCast AI uses historical electricity demand data and machine learning techniques to forecast future electricity requirements, identify peak-load periods, and compare forecasting algorithms.
